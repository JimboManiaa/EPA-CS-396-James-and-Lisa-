The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) aims to protect both human and environmental health through the collection of Clean Air Markets Program Data (CAMPD). EPA collects a series of relevant, comprehensive data related to power plant emission in the United States, such as:

  • Emission data (CO2, NOx, SO2, and mercury)
  • Compliance data
  • Allowance data

EPA provides several free datasets which the public can utilize for research, data management, and pollution tracking. Through CAMPD, communities and citizens can track local pollution sources, monitor compliance with federal clean air laws, and use the public data to hold power companies accountable.
