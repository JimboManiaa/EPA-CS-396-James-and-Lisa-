Explore Environmental Impacts using TRACI 2.2 Data

Through this page, you may estimate the human and environmental health impacts of powerplant facilities across the United States. Using reported CAMPD annual emissions (converted from short tons to kilograms: 1 short ton = 907.18474 kg) and EPA Traci 2.2 characterization factors, we have calculated potential impact through 4 steps:

  • Step 1: The webpage retreives annual emission mass for a specific pollutant (e.g., CO2, SO2, or NOx).
  • Step 2: Mass is converted from short tons into kilograms by multirplying the raw value by the contsant 907.18474.
  • Step 3: The TRACI 2.2 characterization factor values are assigned to the poulltant for the selected impact category (e.g., global warming, acidification, or smog formation).
  • Step 4: The final impact score is calculated by multiplying the poluutant mass in kilograms by the TRACI factor value.
