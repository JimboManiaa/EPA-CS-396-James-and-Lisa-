List of Facilities

The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) provides detailed information about power plant facilities across the United States. Facilities must follow a set of rules to keep the air, water, and land clean. In cooperation with EPA, the Facility Registry Service (FRS) shares this recorded information with governments, businesses, and the public so that citizens can learn about their area.

H. L. Spurlock

H. L. Spurlock is a coal-fired power facility in Mason County, Kentucky. The plant is registered as EIA plant ID 6041. East Kentucky Power Cooperative Inc. is registered as the owner and plant entity/operator. (Ask the Grid)
